## Markify v1.1.0-beta — OCR for images, scanned PDFs and Office documents

Markify now reads text from **images**, **scanned PDFs** and **images inside Word and PowerPoint files**, 100 % locally (no cloud, no account). Still a beta.

### ✨ What's new

- **Images → Markdown:** PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP, BMP and TIFF are converted with local OCR (Spanish and English, accents and ¿ ¡ included).
- **Scanned PDFs:** each page is analysed on its own. Text pages are extracted as before; scanned pages are recognized (rotated pages are straightened and two-column layouts are read column by column); figures with text inside a text page are read too. Tested with a 227-page scan.
- **Images inside DOCX and PPTX:** screenshots, scanned pages pasted into Word and diagrams are read, and their text appears where the image was. A real alt text is kept as an italic line. Logos, icons and repeated images are skipped.
- **A bad page never fails the whole file:** it is replaced by a short comment and the file shows a ⚠ warning icon with the details.
- **Old `.doc` / `.ppt` files** are now rejected with a clear message ("save it as .docx / .pptx") instead of a generic error.
- **Cleaner output:** DOCX and PPTX no longer contain broken `![](…)` image links, and the rendered preview hides the HTML comments (slide markers become a separator line).
- **Faster and steadier:** later launches take about 2 s (the first one after installing takes 30–40 s while macOS verifies the files), cancelling a transcription works between Whisper's windows, and the model check is reliable.
- **Third-party licenses** are included in the app (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`).

### 🧰 Before you start

- **FFmpeg is required for audio and video** (it is not bundled): `brew install ffmpeg`. Documents, images and OCR work without it.
- **The Whisper model (~145 MB)** is downloaded the first time you transcribe audio. Everything else works offline.
- **The first time macOS opens the app** it may say it cannot verify the developer (the app is not notarized). Drag Markify to Applications, then right-click it → **Open** → **Open**. If that option is missing: *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*.

### ⚠️ Known limitations

- OCR reads text only (no tables, headings or handwriting) and is tuned for Spanish and English.
- In DOCX/PPTX only images shown at 1 % of the page or more are read; text of fewer than 6 letters/digits inside an image is ignored; EMF/WMF/SVG images are skipped with a warning; crops and rotations applied inside the document are not applied.
- Password-protected PDFs are rejected. Only the first frame of a multi-frame TIFF is read.
- Audio language is detected automatically; very short or synthetic voices can be misdetected.
